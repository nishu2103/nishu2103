## 👋 Hey, I'm Nishanth

I'm a CS student at **Nirma University** (Integrated B.Tech CSE + MBA) who spends most of his time making drones smarter — and getting them to work *together*.

I'm a Software Lead of **Team Arrow**, a competitive autonomous drone team where we design, build, and fly fully autonomous UAV systems from scratch. My focus is on the stuff that happens *between* the drones: how they communicate, coordinate, and make decisions as a collective — without any human input.

Most of my work lives under NDA(Non-Disclosure Agreement), but what I can tell you is — getting a swarm of autonomous drones to talk to each other in real-time and actually *work* in the field is one of the most satisfying engineering challenges I've taken on.

## 🚁 What I Work On at Team Arrow

The code is confidential, but here's a high-level picture of my role:

- **Inter-drone communication** — designing how UAVs exchange telemetry and state in real-time over ad-hoc networks
- **Swarm coordination logic** — distributed decision-making so drones behave as a coherent system, not just a fleet
- **Onboard computing** — deploying perception and control pipelines on Raspberry Pi and NVIDIA Jetson at the edge
- **Autonomous navigation** — end-to-end mission execution with no human in the loop
- **Sensor fusion** — combining multiple data streams for reliable real-world situational awareness
